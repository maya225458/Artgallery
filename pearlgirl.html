Girl with a Pearl Earring

Johannes Vermeer

Year: 1665

Style: Tronie

Museum: Mauritshuis Museum,The Hague,Netherlands

Girl with a Pearl Earring is one of Johannes Vermeer's most famous pintings. It is often called the "Mona Lisa of the north " because of the girl's mysterious expression and captivating gaze.The painting is admired for its beautigul use of light,soft colors, and the striking pearl earring.
