Girl with a Pearl Earring

Artist: Johannes Vermeer

Year of Creation: 1665

Category: Baroque Portrait

Description

Girl with a Pearl Earring is one of the most celebrated paintings by the Dutch artist Johannes Vermeer. Often referred to as the "Mona Lisa of the North," the painting captures a young woman wearing an exotic dress, a blue and gold turban, and a striking pearl earring. The soft lighting, realistic expression, and delicate brushwork make it one of the greatest masterpieces of the Dutch Golden Age.
