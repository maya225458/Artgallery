The Scream

Artist: Edvard Munch

Year of Creation: 1893

Category: Expressionism

Description

The Scream is one of the most recognizable paintings in the world. Created by Norwegian artist Edvard Munch, it portrays a figure standing on a bridge with an expression of fear and anxiety while the sky glows with dramatic colors. The painting symbolizes human emotion, loneliness, and existential anxiety, making it one of the greatest works of Expressionist art.
