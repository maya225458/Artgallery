The Persistence of Memory

Salvador Dali

Year: 1931

Style: Surrealism

Museum: Museum of Modern Art, New York

The Persistence of Memory is one of Salvador Dali's most famous surrealist paintings. It features melting clocks placed in a dream-like landscape,symbolizing the fluid and mysterious nature of time. The artwork encourages viewers to think about reality,dreams,and the passage of time in a different way.
