The Persistence of Memory

Artist: Salvador Dalí

Year of Creation: 1931

Category: Surrealism

Description

The Persistence of Memory is one of Salvador Dalí's most famous surrealist paintings. It features melting clocks draped over objects in a dreamlike landscape, challenging our perception of time and reality. The artwork is celebrated for its imaginative symbolism and remains one of the defining masterpieces of Surrealism.
