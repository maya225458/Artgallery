Mona Lisa

Artist: Leonardo da Vinci

Year of Creation: 1503–1506 (completed around 1517)

Category: Renaissance Portrait

Description

The Mona Lisa is one of the world's most famous paintings. Created by Leonardo da Vinci during the Renaissance, it is admired for the subject's mysterious smile, realistic details, and innovative painting techniques. The portrait is displayed in the Louvre Museum in Paris and attracts millions of visitors every year.
