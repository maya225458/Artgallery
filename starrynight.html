The Starry Night

Artist: Vincent van Gogh

Year of Creation: 1889

Category: Post-Impressionism

Description

The Starry Night is one of Vincent van Gogh's most celebrated paintings. It portrays a peaceful village beneath a dramatic night sky filled with swirling clouds, bright stars, and a glowing crescent moon. Painted while Van Gogh was at the Saint-Paul-de-Mausole asylum in France, the artwork expresses emotion through bold colors and expressive brushstrokes.
