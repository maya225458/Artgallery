Starry Night

Vincent van Gogh

Year: 1889

Style: Post-Impressionism

Museum: Museum of Modern Art, New York

Starry Night is one of the world's most famous paintings. It shows a peaceful village beneath a swirling night sky filled with bright stars and a glowing moon. Van Gogh painted it while staying at an asylum in France.
